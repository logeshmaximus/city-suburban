## 8. Process (shared)
What happens when you call · **From the first call to a warm apartment, in six steps**
- 01 **You call, Rob listens**: Tell us the make of the furnace or condenser and what it's doing. A good description often narrows it down on the phone.

## 9. What changes the price
- Eyebrow (shared): What changes the price
- **H2:** The same repair costs differently in a Victorian house, a two-flat and a loft
- Intro (shared): We don't publish a price book, and we're not going to invent one here. What we can show you is what moves the number.
- Median (shared): **$6,807**, Median reported value of 81 central-furnace replacement permits in Chicago over 24 months. That's the whole project's construction value, not a quote.
- Factor · Why it changes the work:
  1. **Landmark district**: Much of Wicker Park is a Chicago Landmark District, so a new condenser or vent in view of the street can need Landmarks review.
  2. **Boiler or forced air**: The big houses on Hoyne and Pierce, and many two-flats, still heat with a boiler and radiators, a different job from a furnace.
  3. **Condo conversions**: Many Wicker Park two-flats are now condos, so one boiler can serve every unit and the owners agree the repair between them.
  4. **Cooling a radiator house**: Victorian houses in the historic district were built for radiators, not ducts, so cooling usually means ductless mini-splits.
  5. **Lofts above the shops**: Lofts over Milwaukee Avenue and Division run splits or rooftop units, so getting onto the roof sets the work.
  6. **Repair or replace**: The biggest swing of all. Where the unit has years left, we say so.

## 10. How to choose
- **H2:** Five questions that sort Wicker Park HVAC companies
- Eyebrow and note (shared): How to choose · Ask them of anyone you call, including us.
  - Q1 **Who diagnoses, and who quotes?** If they're different people, ask who owns the outcome. Here it's Rob for both.
  - Q2 **Is the technician on commission?** Commission on replacements changes what "your furnace is done" means.
  - Q3 **Does a permit count mean anything here?** Not for repairs. Chicago doesn't permit HVAC repair or maintenance, so a permit count only measures installs.
  - Q4 **What do the newest reviews say?** Read the last 20, not the top 5. Look for names and specifics.
  - Q5 **Will they tell you to keep it?** Ask if the unit has years left. Our reviewers record hearing "yes".

## 15. FAQ
- Eyebrow (shared): Questions
- **H2:** Wicker Park HVAC, answered
  1. **Does the old boiler in my Wicker Park house need replacing?** Often it doesn't. We diagnose first, and where the boiler has years left, we say so. Replacement is quoted only when repairs stop making sense.
  2. **How far is your shop from Wicker Park?** Very close. The shop is at 1225 N Cleaver St, a fifth of a mile east of Ashland Avenue, just north of Division, and Rob runs the calls from there.
  3. **Can you add air conditioning to a house heated by radiators?** Usually, yes. A radiator-heated house has no ducts for central air, so cooling usually means ductless mini-splits. In the landmark district, plan where the outdoor unit goes first.
  4. **Does HVAC repair in Chicago need a permit?** (shared) No. Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling. Permits cover installs and construction, so a company's permit count says little about its repair work.
  5. **What are your hours?** (shared) Monday to Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 6:00 AM to 3:30 PM. Call (773) 238-3838.
