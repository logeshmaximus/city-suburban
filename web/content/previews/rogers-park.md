## 8. Process (shared)

- Factor · Why it changes the work:
  1. **One boiler, many units**: In East Rogers Park's courtyard buildings one boiler heats every apartment, so the owner or association signs off first.
  2. **Landlord or owner**: Rogers Park has many rental buildings, so the landlord books and approves the repair. Tell us who owns the system.
  3. **Cooling a radiator flat**: Vintage Rogers Park apartments heated by radiators have no ducts, so cooling means window units or ductless mini-splits.
  4. **Building access**: Towers on Sheridan Road and courtyard buildings set the access: a key from management, service hours, the boiler room.
  5. **Shops on Morse and Clark**: Restaurants and shops run rooftop units and make-up air, so visits are booked around opening hours.
  6. **Repair or replace**: The biggest swing of all. Where the unit has years left, we say so.

## 10. How to choose
- **H2:** Five questions that sort Rogers Park HVAC companies
- Eyebrow and note (shared): How to choose · Ask them of anyone you call, including us.
  - Q1 **Who diagnoses, and who quotes?** If they're different people, ask who owns the outcome. Here it's Rob for both.
  - Q2 **Is the technician on commission?** Commission on replacements changes what "your furnace is done" means.
  - Q3 **Does a permit count mean anything here?** Not for repairs. Chicago doesn't permit HVAC repair or maintenance, so a permit count only measures installs.
  - Q4 **What do the newest reviews say?** Read the last 20, not the top 5. Look for names and specifics.
  - Q5 **Will they tell you to keep it?** Ask if the unit has years left. Our reviewers record hearing "yes".

## 15. FAQ
- Eyebrow (shared): Questions
- **H2:** Rogers Park HVAC, answered
  1. **Our courtyard building's boiler keeps failing. Does it need replacing?** Not always. We diagnose first, and where the boiler has years left, we say so. Replacement is quoted only when repairs stop making sense for the building.
  2. **Do you come as far north as Rogers Park?** Yes. The shop is at 1225 N Cleaver St in West Town, about seven miles south, and Rob runs the calls from there. Call (773) 238-3838 to book.
  3. **Can I get air conditioning in a radiator-heated apartment?** Usually, yes. Radiator-heated apartments have no ducts for central air, so it means a window unit or a ductless mini-split. In a rental, the landlord approves it first.
  4. **Does HVAC repair in Chicago need a permit?** (shared) No. Chicago doesn't require a permit to repair, diagnose or maintain heating and cooling. Permits cover installs and construction, so a company's permit count says little about its repair work.
  5. **What are your hours?** (shared) Monday to Friday, 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 6:00 AM to 3:30 PM. Call (773) 238-3838.
